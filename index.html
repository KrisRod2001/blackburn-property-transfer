Conveyancing Services in Blackburn

First Class Legal offers end-to-end conveyancing services designed to suit Blackburn’s suburban property market. Their services accommodate standard residential transactions as well as more complex ownership structures.

Property Sales and Purchases

Assistance with buying and selling property in personal names, company structures, family trusts, and SMSFs. This includes auction purchases, private sales, and related-party transfers.

Contract Review and Preparation

Detailed review and preparation of Contracts of Sale and Section 32 Vendor Statements. This includes identifying easements, restrictive covenants, zoning controls, and special conditions that may affect the property.

Searches and Due Diligence

Property searches conducted through Landata and relevant authorities to confirm title details, encumbrances, and planning information before settlement.

Electronic Settlements (PEXA)

Electronic settlements via PEXA allow for secure, paperless title transfers and accurate financial adjustments for council rates, water rates, and owners corporation fees.

Complex and Non-Standard Transactions

Support for refinances, subdivisions, consolidations, and transactions involving SMSFs or trusts, with attention to legal and compliance considerations.

Client Guidance and Post-Settlement Support

Advice on stamp duty concessions, first-home buyer entitlements, and post-settlement notifications to councils and authorities to ensure records are correctly updated.
